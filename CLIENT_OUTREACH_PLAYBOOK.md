# Client Outreach Playbook: Getting Your First AI Projects

## 🎯 Your Outreach Channels (Ranked by Speed-to-Revenue)

---

### 1. 💬 WhatsApp & Direct DMs (Fastest — Results in 1-3 days)

**Target:** Local businesses you can see around you — clinics, coaching centers, gyms, restaurants, real estate agents, small e-commerce shops.

**Message Template:**
```
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] — I help businesses like yours save time using AI.

I noticed [specific observation about their business — e.g., "your clinic doesn't have online booking" or "your Instagram replies seem delayed"].

I can build a WhatsApp chatbot that handles [specific thing] automatically — no coding needed on your end.

Would you be open to a quick 10-min call? I'll show you exactly how it works. No charge for the consultation.

Here's my portfolio: [your portfolio URL]
```

**Volume:** DM 10-15 businesses per day. Expect 2-3 replies, 1 meeting.

---

### 2. 🌐 Fiverr & Upwork (Revenue in 3-7 days)

**Gig Ideas to Post:**
| Gig Title | Starting Price |
|---|---|
| "I will build a custom AI chatbot for your website" | ₹2,500 |
| "I will automate your business workflows with AI" | ₹4,000 |
| "I will write SEO blog posts using AI + human editing" | ₹1,500 |
| "I will create a WhatsApp auto-reply bot for your business" | ₹3,000 |
| "I will build an AI-powered customer support system" | ₹5,000 |

**Pro Tips:**
- Use your portfolio page as the gig gallery (take screenshots)
- Price low for first 3 gigs to get reviews, then raise 2-3x
- Respond to buyer requests within 5 minutes (Fiverr rewards fast replies)
- Add a video introduction — gigs with videos get 40% more orders

---

### 3. 🐦 Twitter/X & LinkedIn (Revenue in 5-10 days)

**Strategy:** Build in public + offer free value.

**Post Templates:**

**Day 1 — Introduction:**
> "I just built a WhatsApp chatbot that handles 60% of a dental clinic's calls automatically.
>
> If you run a small business and want to see how AI can save you 10+ hours/week, DM me.
>
> I'll analyze your business for free."

**Day 3 — Case Study:**
> "A local e-commerce store was losing leads because they took 2+ hours to reply.
>
> I set up an automated AI follow-up system.
>
> Result: 3x more conversions, zero manual work.
>
> Here's how I built it: [thread]"

**Day 5 — Free Value:**
> "5 things every small business should automate with AI (but doesn't):
>
> 1. Customer FAQ replies
> 2. Appointment booking
> 3. Lead follow-up emails
> 4. Social media content
> 5. Invoice reminders
>
> I can set up any of these in 48 hours. DM me."

**Posting cadence:** 1 post/day, respond to EVERY comment, DM anyone who engages.

---

### 4. 🤝 Local Networking & Referrals (Revenue in 7-14 days)

**Places to find clients in person:**
- Local business associations / chamber of commerce
- Co-working spaces (offer to automate something for the space itself)
- BNI networking groups
- Startup meetups
- Tech events & hackathons

**Your 30-Second Pitch:**
> "I help businesses save 10+ hours a week using AI. I build chatbots, automate workflows, and create content — all powered by AI. My last client reduced their manual work by 60%. Want me to analyze your business for free?"

---

### 5. 📧 Cold Email (Revenue in 7-14 days)

**Subject Lines That Work:**
- "Quick question about [Company Name]'s customer support"
- "I found a way to save [Company Name] 10 hours/week"
- "Can I automate [specific task] for you? (free analysis)"

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: Quick idea for [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific observation] about [their business/website].

I help businesses like yours automate [specific thing] using AI — 
typically saving 10-20 hours per week.

For example, I recently built a WhatsApp chatbot for a dental clinic 
that reduced their receptionist workload by 60%.

Would you be open to a quick 15-min call where I show you exactly 
what AI can do for your business? No charge, no strings.

[Your name]
[Portfolio link]
```

**Volume:** Send 20 emails/day. Use tools like Hunter.io to find emails.

---

### 6. 🏪 Online Communities (Revenue in 5-10 days)

**Where to offer help:**
- Reddit: r/smallbusiness, r/entrepreneur, r/Fiverr, r/forhire
- Facebook Groups: search for "[your city] business owners" or "small business India"
- Discord: AI & tech communities
- IndieHackers: offer AI services to indie founders

**Approach:** Answer questions → show expertise → offer services in DM. Never spam.
