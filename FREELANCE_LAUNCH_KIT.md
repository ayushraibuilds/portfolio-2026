### Upwork Connects Strategy

**How Connects Work:**

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|

**Tiered Bidding — Don't Treat All Jobs Equally:**

| Tier | Jobs/Day | Connects | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Tier 1: Perfect Fit** | 2-3 | 4-6 each | HIGH — custom proposal, mention specifics, offer free mini-audit |
| ⚡ **Tier 2: Good Match** | 5-7 | 2-4 each | MEDIUM — template + 2-3 customized lines |
| 🎲 **Tier 3: Quick Apply** | 3-5 | 2 each | LOW — template-based, small/easy jobs only |

**Daily budget: ~30-50 Connects (₹375-625/day max)**

**Daily Upwork Routine (45 min — mornings are best):**

1. Filter jobs posted in the **last 1-2 hours**
2. Search keywords: `chatbot`, `automation`, `n8n`, `zapier`, `AI`, `GPT`, `workflow`, `whatsapp bot`, `react website`
3. Apply filters: **Payment verified ✅**, Intermediate-Expert level, $100+ budget
4. Scan 20-30 job posts → pick 10-15 worth applying
5. Apply to **Tier 1 first** (2-3 perfect fits — spend time on these)
6. Then Tier 2 & 3 to fill the rest

**How to Pick Tier 1 Jobs:**
- Client has **verified payment** + **previous hire history**
- Job description is **detailed** (serious buyer)
- Budget matches your pricing
- You've done **exactly this type of work** before
- Posted within **last 2 hours**

**🚫 Jobs to SKIP (save your Connects):**
- No payment method verified
- Unrealistically low budget (< $50 for complex work)
- Vague 1-line descriptions
- 50+ proposals already submitted
- "Looking for long-term partner" with $5/hr budget

```

One good client pays for months of Connects.
```

**Pro Tips to Save Connects:**

2. **Set up job alerts** — get notified instantly for new matching jobs, be first to apply
3. **Buy Freelancer Plus ($14.99/month)** once serious — gives 80 Connects/month + see competitor bids
4. **Respond to invitations** — FREE (no Connects needed), higher conversion rates
